HOW TO PLAY

DRIVE

Drag on the left side of the screen to steer. The car accelerates on its own. Hold the lower right side to brake, and keep holding at a standstill to reverse. The HB button is a handbrake for tight turns.

FLY

The moment you leave the ground, that same drag becomes a flight stick. Drag down for a backflip, up for a front flip, sideways to roll, and diagonal for combos. Hold the right side to stabilize: your rotation settles, the car glides, and you can set it down level.

SCORE

Points stay pending while you are in the air and only bank when you land. Clean landings bank in full and grow the combo. A bad landing loses the whole air, but the car always rights itself and keeps rolling. Nothing stops you. Coins pay 250 each. Take off again within 3 seconds of landing to keep the combo alive.

SCORE ATTACK: one three minute run. Beat your best score, combo, and coin count.

HEAT LADDER: five heats with rising targets. The rim opens at Heat 2, the deck at Heat 3, and from Heat 3 coins also buy you time.

TRACK RUN: a downhill time trial on the Ridgeline Run. The clock starts at 75 seconds and every gate you cross adds 8 more. Stunts and coins still score, but the clock is the headline. Missed gates are allowed, they just cost you the time.

ENDLESS CLIMB: an endless ramp into the sky. It narrows and curves harder the higher you get, and there is nothing off the edges but the long way down. The gold line across the ramp is your best distance. How far can you get?
